⏰ Timing Guidance

Total Time Needed: 60-90 minutes before opening | Staff Required: 2-3 people | Start Time: Arrive 90 minutes before first customer to ensure smooth opening

🔑 1. Arrival & Safety Check (5 min)
First Thing
Unlock and disarm security system
Enter through designated entrance, disarm alarm within 60 seconds, secure keys
Walk-through inspection
Check for any signs of break-in, water damage, pest activity, or maintenance issues. Look for spills, broken equipment, or safety hazards
Verify all closing tasks completed
Check that previous shift completed trash removal, surface cleaning, and equipment shutdown properly
⚙️ 2. Equipment Activation (20-30 min)
Allow warm-up time
Turn on espresso machine FIRST
Check water reservoir is full, verify pressure gauge reading, turn on machine (needs 20-30 min to reach optimal brewing temp of 195-205°F)
Check refrigeration units
Verify temps are 35-38°F for fridges, 0°F for freezers. Check door seals, ensure proper closure overnight
Calibrate grinders
Check grind settings, run test shots, adjust for daily beans. Verify hopper is full and beans are fresh
Test steam wand & clean
Purge steam wand, check for proper pressure and steam quality, clean any milk residue from previous day
Start drip coffee brewers
Turn on all drip brewers, let them warm up. Prepare first batch of drip coffee so it's ready when doors open
Prepare cold brew for service
Transfer cold brew from brewing container to serving pitcher/dispenser. Check volume - do you have enough for the day? Note if you need to start a new batch. Store in refrigerator at bar
Start new cold brew batch if needed
If running low (less than half capacity), start fresh batch: Coarse grind coffee (1:4 to 1:8 ratio), add to brewing container with cold water, stir, cover, refrigerate for 12-24 hours. Date and label
Boot up POS system & test card reader
Turn on POS terminals, verify internet connection, run test transaction, ensure card reader is functioning
Activate ovens, toasters, food warmers
Turn on all food prep equipment, set to proper temperatures, allow adequate warm-up time
📦 3. Espresso Dialing Results (5 min)
Quality Control
Pull test shot and record extraction time
Pull a double shot, time from button press to 2oz output. Target: 25-30 seconds. Record actual time below.
Taste and evaluate shot quality
Assess: Is it sweet and balanced? Any sourness (under-extracted) or bitterness (over-extracted)? Adjust grind if needed and pull another test shot
Confirm final parameters are dialed in
Once espresso tastes great, you're ready to serve. Record final settings above so team knows the baseline for today
📦 4. Inventory Check & Stocking (15 min)
Critical for service
Check milk & alternative milk supplies
Verify you have adequate whole milk, oat milk, almond milk, etc. Check expiration dates, bring fresh stock from cooler to bar fridge
Stock syrups & flavor shots
Check levels of vanilla, caramel, hazelnut, seasonal flavors. Refill pumps, wipe down bottles, ensure all are in date
Verify coffee bean inventory
Check both espresso and drip coffee bean levels, ensure hoppers are full, note if running low for reorder
Stock pastry case with fresh items
Display muffins, croissants, cookies, etc. attractively. Check expiration dates, rotate stock FIFO (first in, first out), note sellable quantities
Update POS with pastry quantities
Count each pastry type and update inventory in POS system. This ensures accurate 86-ing when items run out and helps track daily sales by item
Replenish cups, lids, sleeves, straws
Stock all sizes at stations, ensure grab-and-go area is fully stocked, refill napkins and stirrers
Restock condiment bar
Fill sugar, sweeteners, cinnamon, cocoa powder. Refill cream/milk dispensers, clean spills, organize area
Verify cold brew supply & check batch status
Check current cold brew volume in serving container. Is there a batch steeping? Check date/time labels - if it's been 12-24 hours, strain and refrigerate. If low on ready-to-serve cold brew, prioritize starting a new batch
🧹 5. Cleaning & Customer Area Prep (15 min)
First impressions
Wipe down all counters & bar surfaces
Clean espresso bar, register counter, prep stations with food-safe sanitizer. Ensure spotless workspace
Clean & arrange customer seating area
Wipe all tables and chairs, straighten furniture layout, remove any trash, ensure inviting atmosphere
Check & clean restrooms
Ensure toilets are clean, sinks are spotless, restock toilet paper, paper towels, and hand soap. Check floors are mopped
Sweep & mop floors (customer & work areas)
Start with customer-facing areas, then back of house. Check for spills or sticky spots
Empty all trash bins & replace liners
Remove overnight trash from all areas, take to dumpster, install fresh liners in all bins
Clean entrance windows & door
Wipe down glass doors, clean windows inside/out, ensure entryway looks welcoming and professional
🎵 6. Atmosphere Setup & Final Touches (10 min)
Ambiance matters
Turn on all lights (indoor & outdoor)
Activate main lights, accent lighting, outdoor signage. Set to appropriate brightness for time of day
Start music system at appropriate volume
Select morning playlist, set volume so it's audible but allows conversation (60-70 dB max)
Adjust HVAC to comfortable temperature
Set heating/cooling to 68-72°F. Consider outdoor temp and expected crowd size
Set up daily specials boards & signage
Update chalkboards with today's specials, seasonal items, pricing. Ensure all promotional materials are current
Place outdoor A-frame sign & seating (if applicable)
Position sidewalk sign with daily specials, arrange outdoor tables/chairs if weather permits
💰 7. Cash Handling & Systems Check (10 min)
Financial readiness
Count & set register float
Retrieve cash drawer from safe, count starting cash ($200-300 typical), organize bills by denomination, verify count with opening sheet
Run POS system test transaction
Process a test sale, verify receipt printer works, test card reader with real card, ensure mobile payments work
Verify online ordering system is active
Check that mobile/web orders can come through, test tablet/device, ensure pickup area is ready
👥 8. Staff Briefing & Final Walkthrough (5 min)
Before opening
Hold quick team huddle
Review daily specials, any 86'd items, expected busy periods, special events or large orders. Assign positions (bar, register, food)
Taste test drip coffee & cold brew
Sample drip coffee for proper extraction and flavor. Taste cold brew - check it's smooth, not over-steeped or sour. If cold brew tastes off or weak, note it and consider dilution ratio or brewing time for next batch
Final walkthrough: are we ready?
Quick scan of entire space - is everything clean, stocked, turned on? Any last-minute fixes? Ready to unlock doors!
Unlock entrance & flip OPEN sign
Unlock front door, flip sign to OPEN, turn on open neon sign if applicable. Smile - you're open for business! ☕

📝 Pro Tips for Efficient Openings

Time Management: Start with tasks that take longest (espresso machine warm-up, equipment boot-up) so they run in parallel with other prep
Dialing In is Critical: Never skip the espresso dialing section. Recording your results helps the entire team maintain consistency throughout the day. If espresso tastes off mid-shift, they can reference your baseline settings
Team Division: Person 1 handles equipment & coffee prep, Person 2 tackles cleaning & stocking, Person 3 (if available) manages cash/systems and final touches
Quality Control: The dialing process is your quality guarantee to customers - take the time to get it right before opening
Consistency is Key: Follow this checklist every single day to create muscle memory and prevent forgotten tasks
Communication: Use the team huddle to share anything unusual - equipment issues, low inventory, expected rushes from nearby events
Document Issues: Keep a logbook for equipment problems, stock shortages, or maintenance needs - don't rely on memory
