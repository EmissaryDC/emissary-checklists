⏰ Timing Guidance

Total Time Needed: 45-60 minutes after last customer | Staff Required: 2-3 people | Start Time: Begin closing tasks 30 minutes before official closing to ensure smooth finish

👋 1. Final Service & Wind Down (10 min before close)
Last orders
Alert customers to closing time
Give 15-minute and 5-minute warnings politely. "Just to let you know, we'll be closing in 15 minutes." Dim lights slightly at 10 minutes to signal
Stop accepting new orders 10 minutes before close
Politely inform late arrivals of closing time. Offer to-go orders only in final minutes
Clear outdoor seating & bring in signage
Wipe down and stack outdoor furniture if applicable. Bring in A-frame sign, secure outdoor items
Lock doors at closing time & flip CLOSED sign
Lock entrance, flip sign, turn off "OPEN" neon light. Ensure all customers have exited
💰 2. Cash Handling & Financial Closeout (15 min)
Critical security
Close out POS system & run end-of-day report
Process all transactions, print Z-report showing daily sales totals, item counts, payment methods breakdown
Count cash register & verify against POS
Count all bills and coins, separate float amount ($200-300), calculate daily cash sales, note any discrepancies on closing sheet
Prepare bank deposit
Place excess cash (above float) in deposit bag with deposit slip. Record amount on manager log
Secure cash in safe
Place cash drawer with float and deposit bag in safe. Lock safe and verify it's secure. Only manager/shift lead should have combination
Document any discrepancies or issues
Note cash overages/shortages, POS problems, refunds given, comped items on manager logbook
Turn off POS terminals
Shut down all register systems properly, unplug card readers to protect from power surges
⚙️ 3. Equipment Deep Clean & Shutdown (20 min)
Most critical section
Backflush espresso machine
Insert blind basket with cleaning powder, run backflush cycle 5x, rinse thoroughly. Removes coffee oils and residue from group heads
Remove & soak portafilters and baskets
Soak in hot water with espresso cleaner for 15-20 minutes, scrub clean, rinse thoroughly, dry and replace
Clean steam wands thoroughly
Purge steam, soak in milk cleaner solution, scrub with brush, purge again. Wipe down exterior. Place protective covers on wand tips
Wipe down entire espresso machine
Clean exterior, drip tray, countertop around machine. Check drip tray is empty. Polish with microfiber cloth for spotless finish
Empty & clean grinders
Remove remaining beans, brush out burrs and hopper, wipe exterior. Don't wash burrs (coffee oils protect them)
Clean & turn off drip coffee brewers
Empty all pots, rinse brewers, clean decanters with coffee cleaner, wipe down machines, turn off and unplug
Wash all blenders, pitchers, and utensils
Disassemble blenders, wash in 3-compartment sink (wash-rinse-sanitize), air dry or towel dry, reassemble when dry
Clean food prep equipment (toasters, ovens, warmers)
Turn off, let cool, scrape/wipe down, clean crumb trays, sanitize surfaces
Check refrigeration units are functioning
Verify temps are 35-38°F, organize contents, ensure doors seal properly, wipe down handles and exterior
🧹 4. Deep Cleaning - All Areas (20 min)
Essential daily
Sanitize ALL counter surfaces & bar
Spray with food-safe sanitizer, let sit 30 seconds, wipe clean. Include espresso bar, prep areas, register counter, condiment station
Clean & sanitize all customer tables and chairs
Wipe every table with sanitizer, check underneath for gum/spills, wipe chair seats and backs, straighten furniture layout
Clean condiment bar thoroughly
Wipe all dispensers, refill sugar/sweeteners, clean drip trays, restock napkins and stirrers, sanitize entire area
Sweep & mop all floors (front & back of house)
Sweep thoroughly first, then mop with floor cleaner. Start from back, work toward front. Pay attention to corners and under equipment
Empty ALL trash bins & take to dumpster
Remove trash from customer area, restrooms, kitchen, bar area. Take all bags to dumpster. Install fresh liners in all bins
Clean restrooms & restock supplies
Clean toilets, sinks, mirrors, empty trash. Restock toilet paper, paper towels, hand soap. Mop floor. Ensure spotless condition
Wash all dishes & clean sink area
Wash remaining dishes, clean 3-compartment sink, wipe down dish area, take out dish trash, sanitize dish racks
Clean windows & entrance door
Wipe glass doors inside and out, clean entrance windows for fresh look tomorrow morning
📦 5. Inventory Check & Restocking (10 min)
Set up success
Store all perishable food items properly
Wrap pastries, put in labeled containers with date stickers, refrigerate items that need it, discard anything past expiration
Check milk & dairy inventory levels
Note how much milk (whole, oat, almond, etc.) remains. Write down what needs to be ordered on inventory sheet. Check expiration dates
Restock cups, lids, sleeves for opening shift
Ensure all size cups are stocked at stations, refill lids, sleeves, straws. Make opening easier for tomorrow's team
Verify coffee bean supply for tomorrow
Check both espresso and drip coffee bean levels. Note on inventory sheet if running low. Set aside fresh beans for morning brew
Check syrup levels & other supplies
Note any syrups running low, check straws, napkins, to-go bags, other consumables. Add to order list if needed
Complete daily inventory count sheet
Fill out closing inventory form with counts of key items. Note any shortages or items needed for tomorrow. Place in manager folder
🔒 6. Final Checks & Security (5 min)
Before leaving
Turn off all equipment & appliances
Verify espresso machine, grinders, ovens, warmers, brewers are OFF. Unplug non-essential items. Leave only refrigeration running
Turn off music system
Power down audio system to save energy and prepare for silence overnight
Adjust HVAC to overnight setting
Set thermostat to energy-saving mode (60-65°F winter, 78-80°F summer). No need to heat/cool empty space
Complete closing checklist & logbook
Sign and date closing checklist. Note any maintenance issues, inventory needs, or incidents in manager logbook for next shift
Walk through entire space - final inspection
Check all areas one last time: Are all surfaces clean? Floors mopped? Trash out? Equipment off? Nothing forgotten?
Turn off all lights except security lights
Turn off main lights, accent lighting, display case lights. Leave exit signs and minimal security lighting on
Verify all windows & back doors are locked
Check every window and back entrance is secure. Test handles to ensure they're locked
Exit through front door, lock, & arm security system
Set alarm with 60-second exit delay. Exit promptly, lock door from outside, verify it's secure. Test door handle. You're done! 🌙

📝 Pro Tips for Efficient Closings

Start Early: Begin closing tasks 30 minutes before official close time. Clean as you go during last hour of service
Team Division: Person 1 handles equipment cleaning & cash closeout, Person 2 tackles floor/table cleaning & trash, Person 3 does inventory & restocking
Equipment Care is Money: Proper daily cleaning extends espresso machine life by years. A $15K machine replacement vs. 15 minutes of backflushing? Easy choice
Prep for Tomorrow: The opening team should walk into a spotless, fully-stocked, organized space. Respect their morning by doing thorough closing work
Document Everything: Use the manager logbook to note equipment issues, inventory needs, customer complaints, maintenance requests. Don't rely on verbal handoffs
Cash Accuracy Matters: Even small daily discrepancies add up. Count carefully, verify against POS report, investigate variances over $5
Never Rush Security: Take those extra 2 minutes to double-check doors, windows, alarm. A break-in costs far more than overtime
